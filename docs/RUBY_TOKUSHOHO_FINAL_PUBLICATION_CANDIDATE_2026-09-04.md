### Payment Timing / Deadlines
**Credit cards:** Payment processing occurs during order placement. The actual card-account debit/billing date depends on the card issuer's closing and payment cycle.

**Konbini:** Follow the payment number/instructions issued by KOMOJU and pay by the transaction-specific deadline. The currently verified KOMOJU Live expiry configuration is **3 days**. Where a specific deadline is displayed for an order, that displayed transaction deadline controls.

**Merpay:** Continue to the KOMOJU payment flow and complete payment using the Merpay app/instructions. The order is confirmed after successful payment completion.

**Paidy:** Paidy authorization/payment processing occurs when the order is placed. Paidy issues the monthly bill during the 1st–5th of the following month, with payment due on the 27th of that following month. Fees may apply depending on the customer's selected Paidy payment method; bank debit is free.

### Fulfillment
**Delivery:** Normally dispatched within 2–5 days after order confirmation and required payment completion, then delivered on the selected/confirmed delivery date where available. Product-specific or operational exceptions are disclosed before order submission.

**Store pickup:** Collected at Ruby's Cake Delights at the selected/confirmed pickup date and time. Current verified pickup hours are Wednesday–Saturday, 14:00–20:00, subject to the recorded mid-September 2026 recheck and the actual pickup availability shown during ordering.

### Cancellation / Changes
Unless a product/order specifically discloses different terms:

- 48+ hours before scheduled pickup/shipment: eligible for full refund.
- 24–<48 hours: 50% cancellation fee; remaining amount refunded.
- <24 hours, same-day cancellation or no-show: 100% cancellation fee.

Custom, large, seasonal or event products may have separately disclosed earlier deadlines. Changes/cancellation may no longer be possible after preparation begins or after shipment. Refund timing may vary by payment method/provider.

### Returns / Defects
Because products are fresh/perishable food, change-of-mind returns after pickup/delivery are generally not accepted.

For shipped items with defects, damage, severe collapse or wrong items, contact info@rubyscakedelights.shop within 24 hours of delivery with order details and photographs. Ruby's Cake Delights will review the case and may provide an appropriate refund or replacement/reshipment. For pickup orders, apparent problems should be raised before leaving where possible; later-discovered issues should be reported as soon as possible, preferably the same day.
