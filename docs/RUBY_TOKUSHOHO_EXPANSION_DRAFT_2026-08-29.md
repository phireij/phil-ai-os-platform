The new WooCommerce storefront plans to use the official KOMOJU integration; the final page must list only the payment methods actually enabled in production and state their timing/deadlines.

### Fulfillment

**Delivery:** Normally shipped within 2–5 days after payment confirmation and delivered on the selected/confirmed delivery date where available.

**Store pickup:** Collected at Ruby's Cake Delights on the selected/confirmed pickup date and time. Current verified pickup hours are Wednesday–Saturday, 14:00–20:00, subject to the recorded mid-September 2026 recheck.

### Cancellation / Changes

Unless a product/order specifically discloses different terms:

- 48+ hours before scheduled pickup/shipment: eligible for full refund.
- 24–<48 hours: 50% cancellation fee; remaining amount refunded.
- <24 hours, same-day cancellation or no-show: 100% cancellation fee.

Custom, large, seasonal or special-event orders may use a separately disclosed earlier deadline. Changes/cancellations may no longer be possible after preparation begins or after shipment.

### Returns / Defects

Because the products are fresh/perishable food, change-of-mind returns after pickup/delivery are generally not accepted.

For shipped items with defects, damage, severe collapse or wrong items, customers should contact `info@rubyscakedelights.shop` within 24 hours of delivery with order details and photographs. Ruby's Cake Delights will review the case and may provide an appropriate refund or replacement/reshipment.

For store pickup, apparent problems should be raised before leaving where possible; later-discovered issues should be reported as soon as possible, preferably the same day.
