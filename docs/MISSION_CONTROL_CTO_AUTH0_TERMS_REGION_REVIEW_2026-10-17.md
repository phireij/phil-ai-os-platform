# MC-021 — Auth0 Free terms and region preflight

**Status (17 October 2026):** public-document review only. Auth0 Free has been selected for a non-production identity proof with a US$0/month Auth0-plan ceiling, but no account, tenant, credential, application, API, tunnel, or connection has been created. This is an engineering and procurement preflight, not legal advice or acceptance of provider terms.

## Findings relevant to the owner's decision

1. [Auth0's sign-up page](https://auth0.com/signup) says continuing accepts Okta's [Auth0 Self Service Product Specific Schedule](https://www.okta.com/legal/auth0-pss-self-service/) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.okta.com/legal/privacy-policy/). Sign-up is therefore a separate terms-acceptance action. The page says no credit card is required for the Free plan; [Auth0 pricing](https://auth0.com/pricing) lists Free at US$0/month. Do not add a payment card, activate a paid tier or paid add-on, or rely on trial-only capabilities for acceptance.
2. The current [Self Service PSS PDF](https://www.okta.com/content/dam/okta---digital/en_us/legal/okta-auth0-self-service-PSS.pdf) treats the Free plan as a Free Trial under the referenced master agreement. Its excess-use clause can require an upgrade payment if applicable usage limits are exceeded. The US$0/month ceiling is therefore **not** a contractual guarantee of zero charges; it requires staying within Free limits, monitoring the Subscription Page, and stopping before any overage or upgrade. The PSS also says Self Service Support is not provided on the Free plan. The owner must review the linked [Okta legal agreements](https://www.okta.com/legal/) and privacy terms before accepting them.
3. [Auth0 tenant creation documentation](https://auth0.com/docs/get-started/auth0-overview/create-tenants) lists Japan (`JP`) among public-cloud regions. It says selecting a locality controls the assigned tenant domain and the region where tenant data is hosted; sub-localities cannot be chosen manually. For an owner working in Japan, **JP is the recommended test-tenant locality**, subject to the owner's explicit choice. The sign-up flow may prompt creation of a tenant immediately; do not proceed until that choice is approved. Tenant names cannot be changed or reused after deletion, so propose the exact name before creation.
4. A JP tenant locality is **not a blanket Japan-only processing guarantee**. Okta's [trust and compliance materials](https://www.okta.com/legal/trustandcompliance/) and applicable data-processing/subprocessor terms need owner review for support, telemetry, transfers, and account data. Limit this proof to the owner's test identity and synthetic Mission Control fixture data; exclude customer/personal operational records and live Mission Control data.

## Bounded non-production proof proposal — requires a new approval

- Create one Auth0 **Free** account/tenant in the owner-approved public-cloud locality (recommended `JP`), with an owner-approved immutable tenant name. Do not add a credit card, custom domain, paid feature, or production user.
- Set up only the minimum test application/API and test credentials needed to check OAuth metadata, authorization-code/PKCE, MCP `resource`-to-audience mapping, exact read scope, JWT signature/issuer/audience/expiry checks, and owner-subject mapping. Use synthetic fixture output only. Trial-only features do not count as Free-plan acceptance.
- Record negative outcomes for wrong owner, wrong audience, missing scope, expired/tampered token, and revoked access before any fixture read. If revocation cannot be made prompt with the selected token lifetime/design, fail the proof rather than assume it works.
- Keep the ChatGPT/Mission Control tunnel, Platform runtime key, live source adapter, deployment, and production connection **out of scope**. Each remains a separate approval gate.
- Confirm the dashboard shows Free/$0 and watch the applicable quotas; stop if the exact flow requires a paid tier, payment method, unsupported region, or an unapproved terms/data-processing change.

**Approval still missing:** the owner has not accepted the terms or chosen a locality and immutable tenant name, and has not authorized account/tenant or credential creation. Do not start sign-up on the strength of this document alone.
